Privacy Policy

Version of November 15, 2026

1. What data we get

  • Telegram data: your ID, name, username and interface language. Telegram passes these when you start the bot or open the mini app.
  • Service data: balance and transaction history, orders (the delivered number and 2FA password), payment invoices, giveaway entries, referral links and activated promo codes.
  • Payment data: the invoice number at the payment provider, the amount and the status. We do not receive or store card details or wallet keys.

We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, location or messages.

2. Why

  • To keep your balance, deliver purchased accounts, and show the login code and purchase history.
  • To verify payments, process refunds and handle support requests.
  • To run giveaways, count invited friends and prevent abuse.
  • To send notifications about orders, top-ups and giveaways. You can stop them by blocking the bot.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. Payment providers (CryptoBot, xRocket, Telegram, the TON network) get only what a payment needs: the amount and invoice number. The marketplace where accounts are bought gets none of your personal data, because purchases are made in the Service's name. Data may be disclosed if the law requires it. We record which number was delivered to whom: if a delivered number is reported for spam or fraud, this record is used to block the offender and may be shared with Telegram or authorities on a lawful request.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on the Service's server, and only the administration can access it. Mini app requests are verified by Telegram's signature, so nobody can open another user's data. Order data is kept while your account in the Service exists, and after that for as long as needed for accounting and disputes.

5. Data on your device

The mini app keeps only your cart in the browser's storage. It is never sent to the server and you can clear it at any time. We use no third-party trackers or advertising cookies.

6. Your rights

You can ask support for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion. After deletion, purchase history and balance cannot be restored. Data that must be kept by law is deleted when that period ends.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available in the mini app.

8. Contact

For questions about your data, contact the Service's support in Telegram.
